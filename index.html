healthcare-ai

NeuroFed AI

Brain tumor classification from MRI scans, trained across three simulated hospitals without sharing any patient images.

  • Federated model reached 63.6% accuracy vs 85.0% centralized under strong non-IID data; I traced the gap to one hospital holding almost all meningioma scans, and tested FedProx as a fix.
  • 7-page Streamlit dashboard that exports results as HL7 FHIR diagnostic reports.
  • CNN trained with FedAvg across hospitals, with privacy analysis and an IEEE-style research paper on the work.
  • TensorFlow/Keras

Quasar Classifier

Sorts sky objects from the SDSS17 survey into stars, galaxies and quasars, with an explanation for every answer.

  • SHAP explanations that show why each object got its label.
  • Model exported to JSON and run in the browser, matching Python predictions on 400 test objects.

air-quality-forecasting

AirSentinel

Forecasts air quality for Delhi-NCR by combining pollution data, satellite fire detections and weather, then gives health alerts.

  • Joins CPCB air quality data, NASA FIRMS stubble-fire data and Open-Meteo weather.
  • LightGBM with a time-based split and backtesting, so the model never sees future data.
  • SHAP shows how much fires vs weather drive each forecast.
